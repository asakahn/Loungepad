Battle.net, Ubisoft Connect, the EA app and the Rockstar Games Launcher join the library.

## New

- **Battle.net games.** Everything you have installed through Battle.net shows up on its own:
  World of Warcraft, Diablo, Overwatch, Hearthstone, StarCraft, Call of Duty, and the other games
  Battle.net sells, such as Sea of Thieves, Avowed and DOOM: The Dark Ages. Play starts the
  Battle.net app for you if it is not running, waits for it to sign in, and launches the game.
  Sign in under Settings → Library → Battle.net account and the games on your account that are
  not installed join the library too, greyed out; pressing one opens it in the Battle.net app,
  ready to install. Diablo II and Warcraft III from before the Battle.net app start directly.
- **Ubisoft Connect games.** Installed games are found on their own and start through Ubisoft
  Connect. Sign in under Settings → Library → Ubisoft Connect account to add the rest of the
  games you own (Loungepad reads them from Ubisoft Connect on your PC, so sign in to Ubisoft
  Connect there once too) and to see your Ubisoft achievements, with their pictures.
- **EA app games.** Installed games are found on their own and start through the EA app. Sign in
  under Settings → Library → EA account to add the rest of the games you own, each one installable
  from the library, and to see your EA achievements. EA lists names and unlock dates but no
  pictures or rarity, so those achievements show the trophy instead.
- **Rockstar Games Launcher games.** Grand Theft Auto V, Red Dead Redemption 2, the GTA trilogy,
  L.A. Noire, Max Payne 3, Bully and the rest show up once they are installed, and start through
  the Rockstar Games Launcher, which signs you in first. A Rockstar game you bought on Steam or Epic
  stays that store's game, as before. Rockstar has no way for other apps to list the games you own
  or your achievements, so only installed games appear.
- **One tile per game, whichever stores you have it in.** A game you own on Steam and on Ubisoft
  Connect, or on GOG and the EA app, is one tile, as with the other stores. Manage → Launch with
  picks which copy it starts.
- **The rest of Loungepad knows them too.** The platform filter lists each store once you have a
  game from it, a game's page shows the store it came from, Stats → Achievements lists the two
  new sources, the first-time setup shows the four apps on your PC, Manage → Change executable
  works for them, and a Playnite import matches their games.

Battle.net has no achievements to show: Blizzard keeps no achievement service across its games.

## Fixed

- **The right stick scrolls the release notes.** It used to scroll the library behind them, while
  the D-pad scrolled the notes. Whatever is in front now takes the stick: a question or the Power
  Wheel no longer lets it move the library underneath either.

## Download and install

1. Under **Assets** below, download **Loungepad.exe**. That one file is the whole app.
2. Put it anywhere you like, such as your Desktop, and double-click it.
3. If Windows says it protected your PC, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Already using Loungepad? It updates itself, so there is nothing to download. You can ignore the
other files: Loungepad fetches the input service itself when you turn it on, and the
`-win-x64.zip` is only for copies on version 1.6.3 or older.
